# Privacy Policy — Mistake Tracker

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Mistake Tracker is designed to store user-entered information locally on the device on which the application is used.

## Information Stored by the App

The application may store information entered by the user, including:

- Names or identifiers for people being tracked
- Behavior categories
- Positive and negative behavior entries
- Dates and timestamps
- Notes
- Counts and related tracking information

This information is stored in the browser's local storage on the user's device.

## Information Sent to the Developer

Mistake Tracker does not transmit behavior records, names, notes, or other tracking information to the developer.

The developer does not operate a server or database containing this information and cannot retrieve information stored locally on a user's device.

## Third-Party Data Collection

Mistake Tracker does not intentionally send user-entered tracking data to third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud-storage services.

Normal requests needed to load the app from its hosting provider may expose ordinary technical information such as an IP address and browser request details to that hosting provider, as occurs when visiting most websites.

## Security and Local Storage

Information stored by Mistake Tracker is stored using browser local storage. This storage should not be considered encrypted or secure against someone who has access to the device, browser profile, or other software capable of accessing that browser storage.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices.

## Data Loss

Locally stored information may be lost if the user clears browser or website data, removes the application, resets the device, changes browsers or devices, or experiences a device or software failure.

The developer is not responsible for lost or inaccessible locally stored information.

## Information About Other People

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have any permission or consent required to record information concerning another person and for complying with applicable laws.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes.
